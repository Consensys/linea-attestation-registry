# Proposal Templates

Use the forum's live templates when possible. This page keeps the expected structure in GitBook for reference.

## New chain deployment

Include:

- why the new network matters;
- who will operate the deployment;
- expected maintenance burden;
- contracts, subgraph, SDK, explorer, and docs work required;
- rollout and verification plan.

## New issuer onboarding

Include:

- issuer background;
- schemas to be created or reused;
- portal and module plan;
- expected attestation semantics;
- why the integration benefits the Verax ecosystem.

This matters primarily for allowlisted mainnet environments.

## Protocol improvement proposal

Include:

- problem statement;
- proposed change;
- impact on contracts, SDK, explorer, subgraph, and docs;
- migration or deployment plan;
- risks, open questions, and verification steps.
