# Overview of Governance

Verax governance exists to coordinate a shared protocol that spans contracts, subgraph, SDK, explorer, documentation,
and deployments across several networks.

## Why governance matters here

Unlike a single-application stack, Verax has shared protocol surfaces:

- common registries;
- canonical schemas;
- public infrastructure;
- deployment operations;
- mainnet allowlist policy.

Those shared surfaces create decisions that are broader than one team or one app.

## Governance and product docs

For most developers, governance is background context. The primary documentation paths remain:

- `Build with Verax`
- `Research Verax`

Governance becomes important when you need to:

- understand mainnet issuer onboarding;
- propose a protocol or operational change;
- reason about who maintains shared deployments.

## Live source of truth

Because governance changes faster than static documentation, the community forum should be treated as the live source of
truth for active proposals and current process details.
