# Governance Charter

This charter captures the intended governance principles around Verax. Treat it as a policy reference, not as a
technical specification.

## Principles

### Transparency

Protocol decisions should be discussable in public, with clear rationale and visible tradeoffs.

### Inclusivity

Teams building with Verax, maintaining Verax, or relying on Verax data should be able to participate in shaping its
direction.

### Adaptability

Governance should be able to evolve as deployments, integrations, and protocol requirements change.

### Responsibility

Maintainers and contributors share responsibility for:

- code maintenance;
- deployment operations;
- documentation quality;
- reviewing upgrades and integrations;
- improving protocol safety and discoverability.

## Scope

Governance may cover:

- protocol upgrades;
- new network deployments;
- operational policy;
- mainnet issuer onboarding and allowlist policy;
- community process changes.

For current operational details, confirm on the community forum.
